# 07 — Gaps, risks and roadmap

Priority is based on the stated goal—simple, affordable day-to-day shop operations and proactive customer updates—not on an external feature checklist. **Critical** means blocks safe operation; **Important** means material trust, security, reliability, or scale gap; **Nice-to-have** means optional differentiation. Effort is a rough engineering estimate (S/M/L), not a schedule commitment.
